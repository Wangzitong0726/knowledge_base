模型作答 · 非原文
**关于「收入」：材料未覆盖。** 七条材料中没有任何一处给出 HBM 的收入金额、收入占比或同比增速。材料 1 只说 `In the fourth quarter of 2025, HBM3E 12-high represented the majority of our HBM shipments.` [1]，这是出货结构，不是收入。 **关于「产能」：有定性表述，无数字。** - `Singapore: we broke ground on an HBM advanced packaging facility to meaningfully expand our total advanced packaging capacity beginning in calendar 2027` [3][5] - `Taiwan: we are modernizing our production capacity for DRAM and HBM products to meet rising market demand.` [3][5] 产品/节点侧的相关表述： - `In 2024, we began volume production of our 8-high 24GB HBM3E` [1] - `In 2025, we delivered samples of HBM4 36GB 12-high to multiple key customers` [1] - `Our HBM4 technology is advancing and remains on schedule for volume production in calendar 2026.` [7] - `We are well-positioned with our industry-leading HBM3E technology.` [7] **关于「资本开支」：材料未覆盖。** 没有任何 HBM 相关的资本开支金额、全年 capex 指引或同比变化数字。材料 3 里的 `$10 billion` 是股票回购授权（`the discretionary repurchase of up to $10 billion of our outstanding common stock`）[3]，与资本开支无关，不能挪用。材料 5 只提到政府激励「generally offset a portion of our planned investments」，同样没有金额 [5]。 **缺口小结：** 若要回答收入、产能（bit/晶圆/封装产能数字）、资本开支三项的量化数据，需要补充材料——目前只有封装厂投产时点（`calendar 2027`）[3][5] 和 HBM4 量产时点（`calendar 2026`）[7] 这两个时间锚点。
