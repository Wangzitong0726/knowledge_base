Advanced computing ICs containing co-packaged logic and HBM are not controlled by 3A090.c, though they may be controlled by other ECCNs such as 3A090.a or 3A090.b depending on their Total Processing Performance (TPP) and performance density. This IFR imposes these new controls on HBM stacks under ECCN 3A090.c because these commodities are an important part of the ``production'' process for making advanced computing ICs, which prior to this IFR were not being controlled under ECCN 3A090. In order to more effectively address the national security and foreign policy concerns which are being addressed under ECCN 3A090, BIS added a control on HBM to prevent the PRC, as well as other destinations of concern, from producing advanced computing ICs incorporating HBM.
