## 结论先说 **三星和 SK 海力士最近一期报告的原文，材料未覆盖。** 材料里只有美光的 10-Q（材料 1、2、4、5、6）和佰维存储的年报/半年报（材料 3、7、8），没有三星、SK 海力士任何一份报告的表述。因此「三家」对 HBM 供需与产能表述的共性和差异，**无法回答**；缺的是三星、SK 海力士自己报告中的相关段落。下面只能给出美光一侧的内容，并标注美光自身的期次差异。 --- ## 一、美光最近一期（材料 6，10-Q 2026-06-25）的表述 - 产能/晶圆消耗：`HBM requires a higher number of wafers and more cleanroom space to produce the same number of bits as conventional DRAM in the same technology node` [6] - 供需转换风险：`If demand for HBM weakens and suppliers shift capacity from HBM to conventional DRAM, this could result in a significant increase in conventional DRAM supply` [6] - 价格与业绩：`An oversupplied DRAM market may lead to downward pressure on pricing, which could adversely impact our financial results` [6] - 反向情形：`Conversely, as the demand for DRAM, HBM, or any of our other products has increased and may continue to increase, we may be unable to increase supply sufficiently to meet such demand` [6] 材料 4（2025-12-18）、材料 5（2026-03-19）与材料 6 这三段**逐字一致** [4][5][6]。 ## 二、美光自身各期的差异 **共性**：五个期次都包含同一句关于晶圆消耗的表述，仅材料 1、2 与材料 4、5、6 有一处词差： - 材料 1、2：`HBM requires a higher number of wafers to produce the same number of bits as conventional DRAM in the same technology node` [1][2] - 材料 4、5、6：同句加入 `and more cleanroom space` [4][5][6] **差异**： | 要点 | 材料 1、2 | 材料 4、5、6 | |---|---|---| | 供需失衡的触发条件 | `If we or our competitors increase HBM supply at a rate that outpaces HBM demand, conversion of HBM capacity to supply other DRAM products could result in significant supply-demand imbalances` [1][2] | `If demand for HBM weakens and suppliers shift capacity from HBM to conventional DRAM, this could result in a significant increase in conventional DRAM supply` [4][5][6] | | 客户因素 | `Our product demand forecasts may also be impacted significantly by the strategic actions of our customers` [1][2] | 无此句 [4][5][6] | | 产品规划前置期 | `our ability to successfully introduce new products often requires us to make product specification decisions multiple years in advance of when new products enter the market` [1][2] | 无此句，改为 `Conversely, ... we may be unable to increase supply sufficiently to meet such demand` [4][5][6] | ## 三、附带说明：材料里关于三星、SK 海力士的内容来自第三方 材料 3、7、8 是**佰维存储**的报告，不是三星/SK 海力士的报告。其中涉及三家的数据均为引用 TrendForce、Gartner 等第三方，例如： - `2023 年，SK 海力士以53%的市场份额领先HBM 市场，其次是三星（38%）和美光（9%）` [3] - `2025 年第三季度的DRAM 市场上，SK 海力士、三星、美光、长鑫存储的市场份额分别为33.2%、32.6%、25.7%、5.8%` [7] - `2024 年，SK 海力士继续保持超过50%的份额领先市场，三星以32%~35%的份额次之，美光的份额约为13%~15%` [8] 这些是市场份额数据，并非三家自身对 HBM 供需与产能的表述，也不能用来替代三星、SK 海力士的报告原文。
